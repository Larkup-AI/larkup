---
title: "Pulling from Integrations"
description: "Connect Notion, Google Drive, Slack, and other tools so your AI always has the latest information."
---

Instead of exporting files manually, Larkup can pull content directly from the apps your team already uses. Everything lands in the same knowledge base and is searchable from Chat.

## Available integrations

Open the **Data** page and click the **Integrations** tab. You will see the full list of available connectors.

<Frame caption="The Integrations tab in the Data page">

</Frame>

Current connectors include:

| Service | Category | What gets indexed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notion | Documentation | Pages, databases, and knowledge bases |
| Google Drive | Documentation | Files and shared drives |
| Google Calendar | Communication | Events, schedules, and meetings |
| Slack | Communication | Channels, messages, and DMs |
| Confluence | Documentation | Pages, spaces, and knowledge bases |
| SharePoint | Documentation | Sites, lists, and document libraries |
| Jira | Project Management | Issues, sprints, and projects |
| Linear | Project Management | Issues, projects, and roadmaps |
| Salesforce | CRM | Accounts, contacts, and opportunities |
| OneDrive | Documentation | Files and folders |
| Outlook | Communication | Emails, contacts, and calendars |
| SAP | ERP | Business processes and data |

Don't see what you need? Click **Ask for integration** and the team will prioritize it.

## Connecting a source

Click **Configure** or **Connect** next to any integration. Services like Notion and Google Drive redirect you through OAuth to authorize Larkup. For services that use an API key, paste the key directly in the form.

Once connected, Larkup fetches the content and queues it for indexing. You can monitor progress in the **Knowledge Base** tab.

## Chatting with your connected data

After indexing is complete, switch to **Chat**. Your integration data is now part of the knowledge base. Ask anything and Larkup retrieves the relevant pages, messages, or records and cites the source.

Because Larkup can index again on demand, you can pull fresh content from an integration at any time by returning to the Integrations tab and clicking **Sync**.
